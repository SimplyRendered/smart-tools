Free tool · @smart_tools_every_week · simplyrendered.github.io/smart-tools · built with ClaudePlain projection geometry: the viewer stands at the station point, the picture plane is the paper, and a corner of the building touches it so that edge is true size. A point at plan (x, y) and height z projects to u = x·S ÷ (y + S), v = (z − E)·S ÷ (y + S). The vanishing points of walls turned θ and 90° − θ to the picture plane lie on the horizon at S ÷ tan θ and S × tan θ from the centre of vision. Keeping the subject inside a 60° cone of vision is the usual drawing rule; the cone angle is editable.
