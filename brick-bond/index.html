Free tool · @smart_tools_every_week · simplyrendered.github.io/smart-tools · built with ClaudeSet out in quarter-brick modules: ¼ brick = (length + joint) ÷ 4, header = 2 modules, queen closer = 1, three-quarter bat = 3, stretcher = 4 (works when brick width = (length − joint) ÷ 2, true of IS 1077 230 × 110 and 190 × 90 bricks and UK 215 × 102.5). English and Flemish bond put a queen closer after the quoin header so vertical joints break by a quarter brick; stretcher bond laps by half a brick, header bond starts alternate courses with a three-quarter bat (standard brickwork practice, e.g. W. B. McKay, Building Construction Vol. 1; Barry, The Construction of Buildings). A straight joint is a vertical joint that lines up in two courses one above the other. Brick count from wall volume; add wastage.
