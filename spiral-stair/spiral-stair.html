Default limits follow IRC 2018 R311.7.10.1 for spiral stairs (clear width 660 mm, tread 190 mm at 12 in from the narrow edge, riser at most 241 mm, headroom 1982 mm), as quoted in a municipal residential stair guide. All limits are editable: check your own code (NBC 2016 Part 4 for egress stairs) and the manufacturer for a kit stair.
