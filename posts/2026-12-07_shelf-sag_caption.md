18 mm MDF for a 900 mm bookshelf? ❌
With 45 kg of books per metre it sags 9.4 mm, and 14 mm after a few years. The target is 1.5 mm: span it 490 mm or stiffen it ✅
Shelf Sag Check gives sag, longest span and thickness for any board. Free, no sign-up, works offline.
