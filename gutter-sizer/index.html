Gutter capacity by Manning, V = (1/n) R^(2/3) S^(1/2), with n: PVC 0.010, galvanized iron 0.016, concrete 0.013, copper 0.011 (typical textbook values). Downpipe inlet = the lesser of a broad-crested weir (Q = 1.45 · πD · h^1.5) and an orifice (Q = 0.6 · A · √(2gh)), h = flow depth in the gutter. Rainfall intensity is your design value: take it from your local rainfall data or drainage code. Preliminary sizing, confirm with the manufacturer.
