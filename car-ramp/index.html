Free tool · @smart_tools_every_week · simplyrendered.github.io/smart-tools · built with ClaudeGeometry only: a rigid car on two wheel contact points, flat underbody between the axles, overhang tips set by the approach and departure angles. Each transition runs at half the ramp gradient, the usual way it is drawn. The car presets are typical values, not a model: enter your design vehicle's wheelbase, overhangs, clearance and angles from its data sheet. The slope limit is yours to set from the local bylaw. A preliminary check, not a swept-path or headroom study.
