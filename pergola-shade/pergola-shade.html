Free tool · @smart_tools_every_week · simplyrendered.github.io/smart-tools · built with ClaudeSun position: NOAA solar algorithm (±1°). Profile angle across the slats: tan θ = tan(altitude) ÷ |cos(sun azimuth − slat normal)|. Shadow of one slat on the ground = width + depth ÷ tan θ; shade = that ÷ (width + gap), capped at 100%. Direct sun only, flat slats on a level grid; sky light and slat reflections are not counted.
