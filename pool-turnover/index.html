Pump flow Q = volume ÷ turnover time. Filter area = Q ÷ filtration rate; filter Ø from the area per filter. Pipe bore d = √(4Q ÷ πv). All first principles. Turnover time, filtration rate and pipe velocity are editable: take them from your local health code, pool consultant or the filter maker's data sheet.
