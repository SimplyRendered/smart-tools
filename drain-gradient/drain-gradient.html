Velocity V = (1/n) R^(2/3) S^(1/2) (Manning), R = hydraulic radius, solved for part-full flow in a circular pipe. n: PVC 0.010, cast iron 0.012, concrete / stoneware 0.013 (typical textbook values). Self-cleansing velocity of about 0.75 m/s and a 3.0 m/s ceiling are commonly quoted for building drains (NBC 2016 Part 9); confirm gradients, minimum cover and manhole spacing with your local code.
