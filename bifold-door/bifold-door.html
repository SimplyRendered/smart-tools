Fully folded, each stack takes its panel count × panel thickness plus the hinge gaps along the opening, and sticks out by about one panel width. While folding at angle a, each panel takes w·cos a + t·sin a along the track. The defaults (frame 60 mm, panel 70 mm thick, 10 mm tolerance, 3 mm gap, 500 to 1,100 mm panels) are typical aluminium values, not a standard: use your system maker's data sheet. A lead (traffic) door that opens on its own needs an odd number of panels on that side.
