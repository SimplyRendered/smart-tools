Stack: Q = Cd·A·√(2·g·h·(Ti−To)/Ti), Cd 0.65 (ASHRAE). Two openings combine as 1/A² = 1/A₁² + 1/A₂². Wind + stack: Q = √(Qw² + Qs²) (Awbi, CIBSE). Required rates: 2024 IMC Table 403.3.1.1 (office 5 cfm/person + 0.06 cfm/ft², classroom 10 cfm/person + 0.12 cfm/ft²) and §403.3.2.1 (dwelling 0.03 cfm/ft² + 7.5 cfm × (bedrooms + 1)). Wind speed is the speed at the window, not the weather-station value. A design estimate, not a CFD result.
