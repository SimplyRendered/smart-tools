    Free tool · @smart_tools_every_week · simplyrendered.github.io/smart-tools · built with ClaudeMinimum character height (uppercase I, measured from the baseline) from 2010 ADA Standards Table 703.5.5: baseline 40–70 in: 5/8 in, plus 1/8 in per ft of viewing distance over 72 in; over 70–120 in: 2 in, plus 1/8 in per ft over 180 in; over 120 in: 3 in, plus 1/8 in per ft over 21 ft (applied pro-rata here). Baseline at least 40 in (1015 mm) above the floor (703.5.6). Viewing distance = horizontal distance to the nearest point people can stand. The '1 in per 10 ft' rule of thumb is editable. Check your local code (e.g. Harmonised Guidelines 2021 in India) too.
