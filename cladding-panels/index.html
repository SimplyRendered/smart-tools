Flat blank = panel + 2 × return (the fold for a cassette panel). Blanks per sheet = the better of the two orientations in a straight grid cut. Sheets = panels ÷ blanks per sheet, rounded up. All first principles; stock sheet size, return and joint are editable, so check them with your panel supplier.
