Module = brick face u + gap g; open area = g ÷ (u + g); bearing each side = (u − g) ÷ 2; bricks per m² = 10⁶ ÷ ((u + g)(h + joint)). Brick sizes: IS 1077 modular 190 × 90 × 90 and common 230 × 110 × 75. Minimum bearing is your choice (editable); check slender or tall panels with an engineer.
